Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide Spendly to you. This includes:

  • Account details such as your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and general usage patterns, used to keep the Service working reliably.

We do not collect payment card details or any financial account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate and improve the Service. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Display your expenses, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Respond to your requests and provide support.
  • Understand how the Service is used so we can fix issues and improve features.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored on servers used to run the Service and is retained for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Once deleted, your expense records cannot be recovered.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party providers for hosting, infrastructure, or analytics. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are expected to handle it responsibly.

Our website may also load external resources such as web fonts. These services may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, as part of serving those resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that we do not control.

5. Your Choices

You can view, update, or delete the expense records you have entered at any time through the Service. You may also request that your account and personal data be removed by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the details provided on our website.
